Office Sathi — अनुवाद (Translate) · Translation engine and language models
==========================================================================

Office Sathi translates on the device, without sending anything anywhere. It uses:

1. Bergamot translator (WebAssembly build), from the npm package @browsermt/bergamot-translator 0.4.9
   (https://github.com/browsermt/bergamot-translator), licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.
   Files: bergamot-translator-worker.js, bergamot-translator-worker.wasm, translator-worker.js.
   One change in translator-worker.js: the WebAssembly file is asked for by its plain name
   ('bergamot-translator-worker.wasm') instead of new URL('./bergamot-translator-worker.wasm', self.location), because
   Office Sathi runs the worker from memory (a blob), where that URL cannot be built. Office Sathi hands the file over.

2. Firefox Translations models by Mozilla (https://github.com/mozilla/firefox-translations-models, trained with
   https://github.com/mozilla/translations), licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0, unchanged.
   English → Hindi is inside the app; every other direction is a language pack downloaded in the app
